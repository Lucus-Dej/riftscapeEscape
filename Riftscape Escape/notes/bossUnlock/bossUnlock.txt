most secret levels can be unlocked via the completion of 3 challenges

slay the rifter king with a fully charged ability
slay a boss with only the ability
complete a run while under the effect of X rune

Veribrose
Slay the Rifter King while possessing a fully upgraded circle
Slay a boss while standing in the circle the entire time
Complete a run while under "Veribrose's Rune"

Torzol
Slay the Rifter King while possessing a fully upgraded minion
Slay a boss with only the minion
Complete a run while under "Torzol's Rune"

Krost
Slay the Rifter King while possessing a fully upgraded dodge
Slay a boss with only the dodge
Complete a run while under "Krost's Rune"

Virst
Slay the Rifter King while possessing a fully upgraded crystal bomb
Slay a boss with only the crystal bomb
Complete a run while under "Virst's Rune"

Sifter
Slay the Rifter King while possessing a fully upgraded sword
Slay a boss with only the Sword
Complete a run while under "Sifter's Rune"
